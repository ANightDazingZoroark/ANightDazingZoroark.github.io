# Rifted Vessels
<img src="/images/rifted_vessels.png" alt="Rifted Vessels" class="image-left" width="128"/>

Rifted Vessels is a [Rifted Valkyrium](/rifted-valkyrium/) addon that serves as a simple ship building mod! Think of it as backport of Eureka Ships to 1.12.2 or a reimagining of DaVinci's vessels.
