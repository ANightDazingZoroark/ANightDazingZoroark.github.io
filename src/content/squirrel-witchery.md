# Squirrel Witchery
<img src="/images/squirrel_witchery.png" alt="Squirrel Witchery" class="image-left" width="128"/>

Squirrel Witchery is a Thaumcraft 6 addon centered around the VTuber [Ayunda Risu](https://www.youtube.com/@ayundarisu) (aka my oshi). Play as her witch persona Ayunda, but as a thaumaturge, studying the thaumaturgical properties of a new form of essentia known as Risunium…

## Links
* [Curseforge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/squirrel-witchery)
* [GitHub Repository](https://github.com/Rift-Modding-Group/Squirrel-Witchery)
