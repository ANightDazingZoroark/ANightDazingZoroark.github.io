# Cenozoic Reborn
<img src="/images/cenozoic_reborn.png" alt="No Peaceful Mode" class="image-left" width="128"/>

Cenozoic Reborn was a Bedrock addon owned by MammothExodus, with a variety of modelers that came together during the pandemic including MammothExodus himself and me as the coder. It was, well, obviously an addon about Cenozoic life.

Unfortunately, with MammothExodus gone, no more updates could be released. For all I know he's happily retired to some shack in the Siberian tundra playing Gmod for the rest of his days.
