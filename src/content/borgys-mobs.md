# Borgy's Mobs
<img src="/images/borgys_mobs.png" alt="No Peaceful Mode" class="image-left" width="128"/>

Borgy's Mobs was a Bedrock addon that added whatever I fancied. This included Half Life aliens, creatures from Philippine mythology, and some original creatures I could think of. 

Not much else really, it was just the most random stuff I could think of. Still, it was discontinued because of lack of interest in continuing, but I might port to Java and continue from there if I had the motivation...
