# Prehistoric Rift
<img src="/images/prehistoric_rift.png" alt="Prehistoric Rift" class="image-left" width="128"/>

Originally released for Bedrock edition as an addon all the way back in 2019, Prehistoric Rift is a Minecraft Java mod that aims to be the definitive dinosaur survival mod. No more dinosaur revival for park building, you will find various prehistoric creatures spawning in your world, which you will have to tame.

Being my oldest and still in continuous mod, it has had a long and storied history. It's main focus are prehistoric creatures not as park decorations or pets, but as necessities for and obstacles to survival. Creature domestication takes inspiration from ARK, where they can hold their own inventories and can be used as battle mounts or companions in combat.

Still though, the mod remains in heavy development, as as of right now, is undergoing a HUGE rewrite. And by huge, I mean a complete rewrite of the code.
